# Driver's Seat 🚗

An interactive playground for new drivers:

- **Autopilot Challenge**: drive a highway sim, engage driver-assist (lane centering + adaptive cruise), and take over fast when it disengages for faded lane lines or construction zones.
- **Levels of Automation**: who's responsible at SAE Levels 0–5.
- **Stopping Distance Lab**: speed, road surface, and distracted-driving effects on stopping distance.
- **Sign & Smarts Quiz**: road-sign shapes and colors plus driver-assist safety questions.
